## Sovereign Grace Music

To: music@sovereigngrace.com

Subject: No-fee lyric partnership enquiry — KOGG Presenter

Hello Sovereign Grace Music team,

We are developing KOGG Presenter through KOGG MEDIA (Africa), with a vision of “Worship without Limits.” It is a lightweight church presentation tool that works in a browser, with offline presentation and private service-library sync.

Could we discuss a no-fee partnership or a small pilot using approved Sovereign Grace lyric texts? A text, JSON, CSV or XML export would be sufficient; we do not require audio recordings or a custom API.

We would like to confirm permission to search and display approved lyrics, split the unchanged words into presentation slides, retain songwriter/publisher credits, save them for offline church use and privately sync a user's library across devices. Please specify any church-level licence, territory, reporting or co-publisher requirements.

We are also exploring an optional church resource community. Public sharing of a song pack would be a separate permission, and we would not assume that private-use access permits redistribution.

Please let us know which titles you can authorise, the attribution you require, and how corrections or removals should be handled. If another rights holder must approve part of this request, a referral would be appreciated.

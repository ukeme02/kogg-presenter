Choose your work mode

Every time the app opens, it asks one simple question: how do you want to work today? Pick once, change anytime from the top bar.

📖

Base Mode

Fresh & fast. The basics load once and the scripture display keeps working even without internet after that — perfect for a one-off projection setup. Nothing to sign, nothing uploaded.

☁️

Sync Mode

Sign in right at the start and your whole library follows you: scriptures, songs, lyrics, schedules, your logo and themes — pulled from the cloud on any device you use, auto-synced as you work.

💾

Local Mode

The hybrid: everything lives and saves on this device — Bible versions, song schedules, lyrics, themes — on Windows, Android, macOS, iOS or Linux, online or off.

Everything a media team actually needs

Built with real services in mind: speed to the projector, zero fuss, and hardware you already own.

📖

Five Bibles, always with you

KJV, BSB, KJV with Strong's numbers, WEB and ASV — downloaded on first load, ready with no internet. Strong's numbers toggle for Bible-study settings.

Account & data safety

An account is optional — it's only there for Sync Mode. In Base and Local Mode the app works fully on your device, forever. When you do create one, it exists for one reason: so your library follows you between devices.

🔒

Designed to be trustable

  • 🔒 Encrypted in transit & at rest — accounts run on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with TLS and row-level security.
  • 🪪 Your data is only yours — a database policy means only your own signed-in session can read or write your row. Not even other users can query it.
  • 🚫 No ads, no analytics, no selling — the app makes no requests beyond your Bible files and your own sync.
  • 📤 Full export, anytime — Tools tab → Export Library gives you your entire library as JSON. You are never locked in.
  • 📜 Open-source texts only — KJV, BSB, WEB, ASV and Strong's numbers are public domain.

Create your free account

We ask for an email and a password — nothing else. The email is used for sign-in and password resets only, and is never shared or used for marketing.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between Base, Sync and Local mode?

Base Mode loads the basics once and keeps scripture display working offline — zero cloud contacts, ideal for a single display setup. Sync Mode signs you in at start; your library pushes and pulls to your account automatically. Local Mode is the hybrid: everything (Bibles, schedules, lyrics, themes) saves on this device only. You can switch modes anytime from the top bar, and none of them stop the app from working offline.

Is it really free? Where's the catch?

No catch: the app is static HTML/JS you can host anywhere for free (Cloudflare Pages), Bibles are public domain, sync uses Supabase's free tier. There is no server cost, no subscription, and nothing is sold — not your data, not anything.

Does it work without internet during a service?

Yes — that's the whole point. Open it once online; the service worker downloads all five Bibles, the starter hymns and the app shell. After that the app runs from your device's cache with Wi-Fi off — in every mode. Voice recognition is the one exception (it's a browser feature that needs connectivity).

Who can see my library if I create an account?

Only you. Row-level security means every request is checked against your own signed-in identity; the database physically refuses queries for other users' rows. Export your full library to JSON anytime.

Can several people share one account?

You can, but it's not designed that way — the library is per-account. Sign in with the same email on the phone and the laptop and they stay in sync.

What about modern translations like NIV or ESV?

They're copyrighted, so bundling isn't allowed by the publishers without a license (which they do grant to churches). KOGG ships only texts that are legally shareable; you can still display such a translation from a website they permit if you have it. Everything else — themes, songs, plans, the interface — has zero restrictions.

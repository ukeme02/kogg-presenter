What it does

📖 Scriptures, offline

Full KJV, WEB and ASV Bibles bundled in the app — 66 books each, always available even with no internet.

🎵 Lyrics library

Split songs into verse/chorus slides automatically. Import lyrics straight from a web page and they're stored locally forever.

🔊 AI voice + auto-advance

One tap and the app speaks each scripture in a natural voice, advancing to the next verse automatically. The voice plays from the projector machine.

🎨 Themes & backgrounds

14 built-in gradient backgrounds, your own images, font, size, weight and alignment — all adjustable live during service.

📺 Lower thirds

Animated name/title banners for speakers — perfect over video in your livestream.

🎛️ OBS-ready

A transparent browser-source URL drops slide text and lower thirds on top of your OBS scenes. Zero plugins.

🗓️ Service plans

Queue songs and scriptures in order, save named plans, run the whole service from one screen or your phone.

⏱️ Countdown & clock

Pre-service countdown timer with clock for the big screen.

How to get it

  1. Phone / tablet / any computer — open the app, then in Chrome or Edge tap Install app / Add to Home Screen. It becomes a real app and works offline.
  2. Android APK — go to pwabuilder.com, paste https://kogg-presenter.pages.dev, and download the signed APK. Sideload it — no Play Store needed.
  3. iPhone / iPad — open the app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Full-screen and offline.
  4. Windows / Mac / Linux — open the app in Chrome or Edge → menu → Install. Standalone window, Start-menu icon, offline.
  5. OBS Studio — add a Browser Source with https://kogg-presenter.pages.dev/display.html?overlay=1 (1920×1080, 30 FPS). It's transparent: text and lower thirds float over your video.

Why another presentation app?

ProPresenter costs ~$29/month. EasyWorship ~$299. OpenLP and Quelea are free but need a desktop install and aren't phone-friendly. KOGG Presenter takes the best ideas — slide-based lyrics, verse-by-verse scriptures, lower thirds, OBS output — and runs them anywhere a browser runs, free, offline, operated from your phone. Light on purpose: ~20 KB of code, no frameworks, no tracking, no accounts.
